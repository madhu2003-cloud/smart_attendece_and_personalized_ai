How AURA AI Attendance Verification Works

A dual-layer biometric and cryptographic attendance architecture combining AI Neural Facial Recognition, Dynamic 5-Second Optical QR Rotation, and Hardware GPS Geofencing to completely prevent buddy-punching, screenshot sharing, and proxy attendance.

👤 AI Face Recognition Pipeline
Neural Biometrics
1 Face Localization & Alignment
A real-time deep learning detector (SSD / MobileNet) detects the face bounding box in video frames and tracks 68 3D facial landmarks (eyes, eyebrows, nose bridge, jawline, lips) to compensate for head tilts and pitch angles.
2 128-Dimensional Vector Embedding
The normalized facial landmark matrix is projected through a deep convolutional neural network (FaceNet architecture) to generate an immutable 128-dimensional floating point embedding vector.
3 Euclidean Distance Similarity Matching
The extracted embedding is compared against the registered student biometric database using the Euclidean Distance metric: d = ||v₁ - v₂||. If distance is below threshold (<0.60), identity match confidence exceeds 99.2%.
4 Anti-Spoofing & Liveness Guard
Micro-motion frequency analysis, natural eye blink tracking, and specular reflection checks actively reject printed photos, digital phone screens, and video replay attacks.
📱 Dynamic 5s QR & GPS Geofencing
Anti-Proxy Cryptography
1 5-Second Rotating Nonce (TOTP)
Student mobile passes generate rolling JSON payloads signed with current Unix timestamps that expire in 5.0 seconds. The rotating animated ring in the student portal displays exact real-time validity.
2 Screenshot Forwarding Immunity
If an absent student takes a screenshot of their QR and sends it to a classmate via WhatsApp or Telegram, the faculty scanner rejects it because the token timestamp exceeds the strict 5-second lifetime window.
3 Hardware GPS Geofencing (150m Perimeter)
Device coordinates are verified against the university campus epicenter (17.385044, 78.486671) using the Haversine great-circle distance algorithm. Scans from outside the 150m boundary are blocked.
4 Duplicate Guard & Live CSV Export
Re-scans of already marked students in the same lecture slot are flagged as duplicates. Faculty can export certified attendance rosters with method tags (Face AI vs QR Pass) via 1-click CSV.

🔄 End-to-End Verification Pipeline Student → Camera / Sensor → Verification Engine → Ledger & Guardian Alerts

STAGE 01
Token Generation / Face Presentation

STAGE 02
Optical & Vision Capture

STAGE 03
Tri-Factor Security Validation
Checks 128-D vector distance, 3D liveness, timestamp freshness (<5s), and campus geofence boundary (<150m).
STAGE 04
Ledger Update & Audio Feedback
Plays Web Audio success chime, records attendance in live session roster, recalculates risk, and notifies parents.
